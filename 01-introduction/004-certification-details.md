# Certification Details

Intro:

- The certificate is available [here](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/certification/certified-kubernetes-application-developer-ckad/)
- It is an Online Exam, with Proctor needed
- The requirements are detailed in the candidate handbook
- The exam is not a multichoice exam, you'll be given a set of tasks to be completed in two hours

Tips:

- You can refer to the k8s documentation at all times
- You could use `kubectl explain` though, to account for slow networks
- Use multiple tabs to switch between different clusters

References:

- Certified Kubernetes Application Developer: https://www.cncf.io/certification/ckad/
- Candidate Handbook: https://www.cncf.io/certification/candidate-handbook
